No intro timing, UI, particles, or documentation polish matters until one tiny Patapon and one giant monster read unmistakably in 3D voxel form. Pick the character-and-scene language to prototype first.

A · ICONIC VOXEL DIORAMA
BIG EYE · FLAT FACE · BLOCK SILHOUETTE

Chunky Iconic Voxels

Recommended. Build characters from deliberate cubes and rectangular prisms. Preserve Patapon’s giant single eye, paper-thin limbs, feather crest, and extreme size contrast. A warm miniature battlefield replaces neon.

B · DENSE VOXEL SCULPTURE
MORE VOXELS · ROUND SILHOUETTES · PBR DETAIL

High-Density Voxel Sculpture

Rounder forms made from many small voxels with richer PBR lighting. More impressive up close, but risks becoming generic voxel art and costs more geometry and iteration time.

C · 2.5D VOXEL PARADE
SIDE VIEW · FLAT DEPTH · STRONG PARADE READ

2.5D Voxel Parade

Strict side-on staging with shallow depth. Strongest resemblance to Patapon’s marching composition, but uses less of the promised 3D space and can feel like a 2D scene extruded into blocks.
