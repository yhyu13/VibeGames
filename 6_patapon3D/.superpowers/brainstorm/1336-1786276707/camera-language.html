Locked direction: full 3D render, 2.5D stage

Characters, terrain, lighting, particles, and animation are true 3D voxel geometry. Gameplay reads as a side-view Patapon procession on a shallow depth plane. Choose how much the camera may break the profile.

A · CINEMATIC PROFILE
LOCKED SIDE CAMERA · SMALL DOLLY/PARALLAX · IMPACT ORBIT ONLY

Cinematic Profile

Recommended. Orthographic or long-lens side view for play. Shallow Z layers create parallax; the camera subtly dollies and may orbit 10–15° only for Moloch’s reveal or command impact.

B · STRICT PROFILE
ORTHOGRAPHIC · NO ORBIT · DEPTH SHOWN BY LIGHT AND PARALLAX

Strict Profile

The camera never rotates away from the side. Most faithful silhouette readability and easiest rhythm staging, but the fully 3D models are less dramatically revealed.

C · DIORAMA ANGLE
PERMANENT 20–25° ANGLE · STRONG DEPTH · WEAKER PROFILE

Angled Diorama

A permanent three-quarter camera showcases geometry and ground depth, but weakens the iconic Patapon side-on procession and can obscure units behind each other.
